Analyst-for-Hire — Treasury Dashboard

An autonomous research agent sells tokenized-stock research reports over x402 (0.25 tUSDC per report, EIP-3009, gasless for the buyer) and sweeps its net revenue into a basket of tokenized stocks under an owner-signed policy. The reports are onchain data summaries — not investment advice — and the agent does not execute trades for users. Everything below is read live from Base Sepolia chain state plus Base mainnet NAV reference data — no server-side bookkeeping.
